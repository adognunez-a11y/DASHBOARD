Monthly net debt with NZ

Net debt = Foreign Suppliers − Advances to DCE NZ − Foreign Customers NZ

2025 was a year of strong deleveraging: net debt fell from US$2,452,799 (year-end 2024) to US$1,357,820 (year-end 2025), −44.6%, by sending advances to NZ that were applied against suppliers in December. Purchases from NZ vs money sent to NZ

In 2025 we sent US$2,407,750 but only purchased US$1,459,359 — we transferred US$948,391 more than we bought, and that surplus was applied against the opening debt (deleveraging).
